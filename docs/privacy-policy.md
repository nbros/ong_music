# Privacy Policy for Ong Music

Effective date: *2026-11-12*

## Introduction
Welcome to **Ong Music**. This privacy policy outlines our commitment to respecting your privacy and explains how we handle any personal information you may provide to us. Since **Ong Music** does not collect any personal information from its users, your privacy is inherently protected.

This policy applies to all versions of **Ong Music**: Android, Windows and the web version.

## What data do we collect?
**Ong Music** does not collect any personal information from its users.

## How do we collect your data?
Since **Ong Music** does not collect any personal data, there is no data collection process in place.

## Data downloaded by the app
**Ong Music** downloads public data from Google Sheets, and video thumbnails from YouTube. Like when you visit any website, these servers see the request (for example your IP address). The app adds no identifier to these requests. The web version also downloads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.gstatic.com), only when a title contains characters its own fonts lack, such as Japanese, Chinese, Korean or Arabic characters, some symbols, or emoji.

The web version, this site and the Windows download are served by GitHub (GitHub Pages and GitHub Releases), which sees these requests in the same way.

The lists show the Twitch names of the viewers who requested songs, as they are written in the public spreadsheets the app reads. The app adds nothing to them and sends them nowhere.

When you open the About tab, and every few minutes while it stays open, the app asks Twitch (api.twitch.tv and static-cdn.jtvnw.net) for Jonathan Ong's public stream schedule and for the preview image of his channel, to show whether he is live and when his next stream starts. Nothing about you is sent beyond what any web request reveals (for example your IP address, the app's or browser's user agent and, in the web version, your browser's language and the address of this site).

In the web version, a highlight's video can also play inside the app: when you tap its large picture, the app opens YouTube's embedded player, in its privacy-enhanced mode, in a frame from youtube-nocookie.com; the app itself contacts no other address of YouTube for it. The player then loads what it needs from Google's servers (the video, its pictures and fonts, and Google's checks against abuse, from addresses such as googlevideo.com, ytimg.com, ggpht.com, gstatic.com, google.com and googleapis.com), and Google receives what it receives from any website showing an embedded YouTube video: your IP address, your browser's details, the address of this site, the cookies your browser already holds for Google's sites (where your browser sends them to embedded content), and what the player records while you watch (see [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)). Nothing of YouTube's player is loaded before you start a video: until then the app only downloads the thumbnails. The Android and Windows apps open videos in YouTube instead.

## Data Storage
As **Ong Music** does not collect any personal data, none is stored by us. The app can store, **only on your device**:

- the downloaded data, so that it does not have to download it again;
- your theme choice;
- the Twitch name you can optionally type to highlight your own song requests;
- the highlights you mark as favourites;
- small technical settings (for example that you closed the "install" hint of the web version);
- on Android and Windows, a technical log file of the app's own activity (start, downloads, errors), to help diagnose problems.

The app itself never sends this data anywhere. The web version keeps the same data in your browser's storage. You can delete it by clearing the app's (or the website's) data, or by uninstalling the app. It stays on your device until you do: we keep no copy of it, so there is nothing for us to retain or to delete.

On Android, the system's own backup, if you have turned it on, can save your theme choice, your Twitch name and your favourites to your Google account, and bring them back when you install the app again or set up a new phone. The downloaded data and the log file are left out of it. You can turn this off in Android's backup settings.

## Data Sharing
Since no data is collected, there is no data to share with third parties.
